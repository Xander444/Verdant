# Verdant

Verdant is a real-time plant care and breeding game. Open `index.html` in a browser to play. There are no dependencies to install or build.

## Host on GitHub Pages

1. Copy the contents of this folder to the root of your GitHub repository. Keep the `assets/` folder alongside `index.html`.
2. In the repository's **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select your publishing branch and **/ (root)**, then save.
3. Open the URL shown in the Pages settings once the deployment is ready.

## Your first plants

- Your starter is a sick Golden Pothos. Water its dry soil, move it to the recommended light, and prune it. Claim your goal reward when it reaches 72% health.
- Use **View plant** or the arrows above the portrait to browse your collection. Shelf lists have their own page controls when there are more than four pots.
- Select **Compare** in Plant Care to see two specimens of the same species side by side. Match the portraits’ maturity to make differences in appearance easier to spot.
- Move plants between the shade, window, and grow lamp. Drag a pot to another shelf, or select the pot and use **Move here**. Each shelf previews how quickly the selected plant's soil will dry there.
- The **Grow lamp** has two spots. It gives plants that need window light during the day or grow-lamp light at night 60% faster growth when their care needs are met. Soil dries 65% faster than at an open window. If a previous save has more than two plants under the lamp, the extras move to the Window.
- Use **Upgrade shelf** to install a humidity tray on any shelf, a shade curtain at the window, or a lamp timer. The timer dims the grow lamp during the day so afternoon shade-lovers can use it. While dimmed, growth is 35% faster with good care and soil dries 30% faster than at an open window.
- Use the **Watering can** to hold and pour until the soil moisture reaches the green range. You can also aim for the same range with **Start pouring** and **Stop pouring** using a mouse, touch, or keyboard.
- Open **Prune** and snip each leaf as it is marked. Choose **Finish pruning** once all the cuts are done. A pruning session takes two to seven snips as the plant grows.
- On a woody plant, open **Train branches** to select and bend a branch. Save the shape to keep the new silhouette.
- Buy a second Common plant. Feed both with your starting fertilizer. You need an open shelf space, five coins, and two plants with at least 72% health and 42% maturity to start a cross.
- Experiment with different pairs, then pot the seedling to reveal the result. A new species or phenotype is added to the Field Guide when you pot it.
- Open **Family tree** in the Breeding Journal or Plant Care to compare portraits of relatives at the same maturity. Relatives stay in the journal after you sell them. Use a saved pair again while both parents remain on your shelves and meet the breeding requirements. Selecting those same plants shows their last potted result.
- Give a plant a **Nickname** and use the star to mark it as a favorite. Choose **Favorites** above the shelves to see only starred plants.
- Open **Repot** to try different pots and compare their appearance. Preview the soil mix in the same dialog. Moisture-retaining soil dries 25% slower and fast-draining soil dries 30% faster than the standard mix.
- Choose **Ready to breed only** to limit both parent dropdowns to plants with enough health and maturity that have finished recovering.
- When two plants of the same species have different phenotypes, you can favor one for the cross. The breeding journal remembers your choice.
- Offspring inherit differences in appearance from their parents, and these differences affect sale value. Cuttings retain the parent's inherited appearance.
- Plants with at least 83% maturity and 75% health can provide a cutting of the same phenotype. A cutting uses one nursery space and costs its parent 8 maturity points. It roots in 20 to 75 minutes, depending on rarity.
- You start with four plant and seedling spaces. Each **Extra shelf space** upgrade doubles that total. Prices start at 28 coins and double with each purchase. Upgrades bought before doubling was introduced count automatically. One previous purchase gives you eight spaces and two give you sixteen.
- Check the rate under a plant’s portrait to see what it earns per hour on display. The swap dialog shows the hourly rates for both the incoming plant and the plants already displayed. You start with two display spots. The Market sells extra spots one at a time up to a maximum of 16, starting at 50 coins and increasing by 30 coins with each purchase. Older purchases above 16 are refunded at their original prices. Select **Swap onto display** to replace one when the display is full. Collect whole display coins in the greenhouse.
- The device's local clock drives light, drying, growth, and germination. Plant care and display earnings catch up for up to seven days between visits. Progress saves in the browser you play in.

## Collector orders and market restocks

- Six collector requests stay active at a time, drawn from 18 request types as your collection and growing history develop. Each delivery cycles that spot to another available type. Requests rotate at local midnight. Grow, prune, or breed a plant to meet a request, then choose it for delivery. Each delivery pays the plant’s sale price plus the posted bonus and immediately adds a replacement request.
- Pin one open order to carry it into subsequent days. Delivering or unpinning it frees the pin.
- The Market refreshes every five minutes. Golden Pothos is always available alongside four rotating Common plants. Uncommon, Rare, and Legendary plants appear in limited quantities, and rarer species appear less often. Buying a species for the first time adds it to the Field Guide.
- The catalog contains 46 species and 240 phenotypes, including six Mythics earned through research.

## Quests, automation, and exhibitions

- The Field Guide shows every species as a silhouette until you discover it. Buy one saved hint per undiscovered species for 25 coins. Breeding hints give a compatible pair, Common plants point to the Market, and Mythics point to their research commission. Revealed entries stay in your guide after selling a plant.
- Open **Quests** to follow 25 quests across collection, breeding, pruning, and collector orders. Existing discoveries and potted offspring count. Earlier order deliveries count when they are still represented in the saved order board.
- The Market sells a **Coin collector** for 120 coins after discovering four species. It transfers whole display coins into your wallet, including during offline catch-up.
- A **Display curator** costs 360 coins and unlocks at ten species. It places your highest-earning plants on display and rechecks them during catch-up. Use Pause to arrange displays yourself. Manually changing a display also pauses the curator.
- At 12 discovered species, buy a **Self-watering greenhouse** for 5,000 coins. It keeps every plant in a safe watering range, maintains humidity, and manages light across all shelves. Existing grow-lamp space and growth bonuses still apply. Flooded soil dries naturally before watering resumes.
- Add a **Fertilizer injector** for 7,500 coins after buying the watering system. It keeps nutrients at 85% or higher with an unlimited built-in fertilizer supply. Both upgrades are permanent, cover new plants automatically, and work during the same seven-day offline catch-up as growth. Pruning stays hands-on. Pause either upgrade in the Market; pausing watering also suspends the injector until watering is enabled again. Stored fertilizer doses stay available for manual care.
- At 20 discovered species, buy up to three **Visitor programme** levels. They cost 600, 1,200, and 2,400 coins and each add 10% to display earnings.
- Discover 12 species to unlock three **Conservatory exhibitions** on the Quests page. Grow different qualifying species, then enter to earn coins and a permanent 5% display-income bonus for the first win in each category. Plants stay in your collection. Enter each category once per local day, with larger collections required as you win repeatedly.

## Mythic plants and advanced collections

- Six **Mythic research** commissions appear on the Quests page. Each requires several collection, breeding, or exhibition milestones, a funding payment of 1,500 to 5,000 coins, and a free nursery space.
- Research awards one sealed seed per commission. It germinates for two hours. Pot it to reveal its species. Names and portraits stay hidden until the first discovery.
- Mythic plants have six phenotypes each. They need more water, humidity, and nutrients and earn higher display income. Their original pots have an aged bronze glaze.
- Mythic plants can breed with another plant of the same species at 75% maturity and 90% health. A cross costs 100 coins and germinates for 90 minutes, followed by six hours of recovery. A Mythic cutting needs 96% maturity and 92% health, costs 125 coins, roots for six hours, and has a 48-hour cooldown.
- After discovering 20 greenhouse species, buy **Grow-lamp extensions** in the Market. Each adds one spot, up to six total. They cost 1,000, 2,000, 4,000, and 8,000 coins.
- **The lost conservatory** exhibition needs three different mature, healthy Mythic species. Repeat wins raise its required collection to six species. Its first ribbon gives the same permanent display-income bonus as other exhibitions.
- The five **Mythic conservator** quests lead from your first research plant to completing all 240 phenotypes. Existing collection quests continue to count the original 40 greenhouse species and 204 phenotypes.

## Artwork and typography

The built-in image generator created the greenhouse banner at `assets/greenhouse.jpg`. The plant illustrations are drawn in the browser and respond to growth, health, and phenotype. As plants mature, they fill out with new foliage, branches, or leaf rings, depending on the species. DM Sans and Instrument Serif are included under their respective SIL Open Font Licenses in `assets/fonts/`.
