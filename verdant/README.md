# Verdant

Verdant is a real-time plant care and breeding game. Open `index.html` in a browser to play. There are no dependencies to install or build.

## Host on GitHub Pages

1. Copy the contents of this folder to the root of your GitHub repository. Keep the `assets/` folder alongside `index.html`.
2. In the repository's **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select your publishing branch and **/ (root)**, then save.
3. Open the URL shown in the Pages settings once the deployment is ready.

## Your first plants

- Your starter is a sick Golden Pothos. Water its dry soil, move it to the recommended light, and prune it. Claim your goal reward when it reaches 72% health.
- Use **View plant** or the arrows above the portrait to browse your collection. Shelf lists have their own page controls when there are more than four pots.
- Move plants between the shade, window, and grow lamp. Drag a pot to another shelf, or select the pot and use **Move here**. Each shelf previews how quickly the selected plant's soil will dry there.
- The **Grow lamp** has two spots. It gives plants that need window light during the day or grow-lamp light at night 60% faster growth when their care needs are met. Soil dries 65% faster than at an open window. If a previous save has more than two plants under the lamp, the extras move to the Window.
- Use **Upgrade shelf** to install a humidity tray on any shelf, a shade curtain at the window, or a lamp timer. The timer dims the grow lamp during the day so afternoon shade-lovers can use it. While dimmed, growth is 35% faster with good care and soil dries 30% faster than at an open window.
- Use the **Watering can** to hold and pour until the soil moisture reaches the green range. You can also aim for the same range with **Start pouring** and **Stop pouring** using a mouse, touch, or keyboard.
- Open **Prune** and snip each leaf as it is marked. Choose **Finish pruning** once all the cuts are done. A pruning session takes two to seven snips as the plant grows.
- On a woody plant, open **Train branches** to select and bend a branch. Save the shape to keep the new silhouette.
- Buy a second Common plant. Feed both with your starting fertilizer. You need an open shelf space, five coins, and two plants with at least 72% health and 42% maturity to start a cross.
- Experiment with different pairs, then pot the seedling to reveal the result. A new species or phenotype is added to the Field Guide when you pot it.
- Open **Family tree** in the Breeding Journal or Plant Care to compare portraits of relatives at the same maturity. Relatives stay in the journal after you sell them. Use a saved pair again while both parents remain on your shelves and meet the breeding requirements. Selecting those same plants shows their last potted result.
- Give a plant a **Nickname** and use the star to mark it as a favorite. Choose **Favorites** above the shelves to see only starred plants.
- Open **Repot** to try different pots and compare their appearance. Preview the soil mix in the same dialog. Moisture-retaining soil dries 25% slower and fast-draining soil dries 30% faster than the standard mix.
- Choose **Ready to breed only** to limit both parent dropdowns to plants with enough health and maturity that have finished recovering.
- When two plants of the same species have different phenotypes, you can favor one for the cross. The breeding journal remembers your choice.
- Offspring inherit differences in appearance from their parents, and these differences affect sale value. Cuttings retain the parent's inherited appearance.
- Plants with at least 83% maturity and 75% health can provide a cutting of the same phenotype. A cutting uses one nursery space and costs its parent 8 maturity points. It roots in 20 to 75 minutes, depending on rarity.
- You start with four plant and seedling spaces. Each **Extra shelf space** upgrade doubles that total. Prices start at 28 coins and double with each purchase. Upgrades bought before doubling was introduced count automatically. One previous purchase gives you eight spaces and two give you sixteen.
- You start with two display spots. The Market sells extra spots one at a time, starting at 50 coins and increasing by 30 coins with each purchase. Select **Swap onto display** to replace one when the display is full. Collect whole display coins in the greenhouse.
- The device's local clock drives light, drying, growth, and germination. Plant care and display earnings catch up for up to seven days between visits. Progress saves in the browser you play in.

## Collector orders and market restocks

- Six collector requests rotate at local midnight. Grow, prune, or breed a plant to meet a request, then choose it for delivery. Each delivery pays the plant’s sale price plus the posted bonus and immediately adds a replacement request.
- Pin one open order to carry it into subsequent days. Delivering or unpinning it frees the pin.
- The Market refreshes every five minutes. Golden Pothos is always available alongside four rotating Common plants. Uncommon, Rare, and Legendary plants appear in limited quantities, and rarer species appear less often. Buying a species for the first time adds it to the Field Guide.
- The catalog contains 40 species, including bonsai and other woody plants.
